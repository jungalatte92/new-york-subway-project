Dr. Jungah Kim

Ph.D. in English from Texas A&M University and Visiting Lecturer at Texas Christian University.

Jungah Kim’s work centers on Victorian studies, with a special focus on materiality and its potential to recover identities marginalized in the nineteenth century. She completed her Ph.D. in English at Texas A&M University in August 2024.

Her book project, “Reclaiming (Lost) Rhythms in Victorian Female Narratives,” examines how Victorian female authors used materiality to create subversive social rhythms. Through the lens of rhythm, she studies works by George Eliot, Anne Brontë, Charlotte Brontë, and neo-Victorian novelist Patricia Park.

Her publications include “Riding Jane Eyre’s Stagecoach Rhythm in Jane Re’s New York Subway” in Victorian Literature and Culture, “Beyond Pojangmacha: Edae Food Carts and the Future of Seoul’s Social Gastronomy” in Food, Culture & Society, and “Nomadic Narrative in Charlotte Brontë’s Villette” in Humanities.
